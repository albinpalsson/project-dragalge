Privacy policy

The Paldea nuzlocke guide (paldeanuzlocke.com). Last updated 20 November 2026.

The Paldea nuzlocke guide is a free web app for tracking a Pokémon Scarlet and Violet Nuzlocke run. This page explains what data the app handles, where it is kept, and what it is used for. The short version: your runs stay in your browser, or, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. The app has no accounts and no server of its own that receives your runs.

Where your runs are stored

You choose where your runs are kept, with the “Stored in” setting on the home page:

  • This browser (the default). Your runs and preferences are saved in your browser’s local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser’s site data can remove them.
  • Google Drive (optional). Your runs are kept in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so you can open them on other devices. Details below.

The two places are separate. Nothing is copied between them unless you export a run and import it yourself.

Google Drive storage

If you choose Google Drive, you sign in with your Google account and allow one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata: “See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive.” This is a hidden folder that belongs to this app only. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

What the app accesses and keeps

  • Your runs. One file in the app’s hidden Drive folder holds your saved runs: game names, settings, encounter and boss results. The app reads and writes this file to load and save your runs.
  • Your account name and email address. The app reads these from Google Drive after you sign in and keeps them in your browser’s local storage, so it can offer “Continue as …” next time. They are not sent anywhere else.
  • A short-lived access token. Google gives the app an access token that lasts about an hour. It is kept only in the open tab’s session storage and is discarded when the tab closes or it expires.

How it is used and shared

  • It is used only to load and save your runs and to show which account you are signed in with.
  • Your browser talks to Google directly. The data does not pass through, and is not stored on, any server operated by this app, so we do not receive or have access to it.
  • It is not shared with anyone, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models, and no person reads it.

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Revoke access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the saved runs: in your Google Drive settings, find this app and choose to delete its hidden app data. Your runs stay there until you do this, even if you switch back to this browser.
  • Forget the account on this device: clear this site’s data in your browser.

Optional usage analytics

If you accept, the app sends basic usage events to Google Analytics: which part of the app you view, when you create a game (and its game version and randomizer mode), when you create your first game in Google Drive, progress milestones (such as a boss battle or encounter outcome), and the app version. These never include your game names or the contents of your saved runs. Google Analytics may use identifiers such as cookies, as described in Google’s own policies. Nothing is sent unless you accept, and you can decline when asked. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

Cookies and local storage

The app uses your browser’s local storage for your runs, preferences (such as theme), your analytics choice, and the remembered Google account. It sets no cookies of its own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
